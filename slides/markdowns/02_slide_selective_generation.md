# slide_selective_generation

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > Selective answering

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > Withholding the least certain answers raises the accuracy of the rest.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Accuracy of the answers that remain as the most uncertain are progressively
  > withheld, ranked by mean token entropy. Withholding half raises the accuracy of the
  > remainder from 45% to 67%. An uninformative score follows the flat line; a perfect
  > ranking follows the upper dashed line. AUROC, reported throughout, measures how far
  > a ranking separates correct answers from incorrect ones.

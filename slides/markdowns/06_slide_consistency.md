# slide_consistency

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > Consistency estimators

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > Disagreement among resampled answers indicates uncertainty.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Several answers are sampled for the same question and their agreement measured,
  > either by shared words or by meaning, with an NLI model testing whether two answers
  > entail each other. Sampling defines tier 3; the NLI model on top defines tier 4.
  > White-box variants additionally weight each sample by its probability.

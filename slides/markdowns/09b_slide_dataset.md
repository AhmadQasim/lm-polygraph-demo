# slide_dataset

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > The evaluation set: BioASQ factoids

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > 1,000 biomedical factoid questions, each with a short reference answer.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Questions are drawn from the BioASQ challenges and retained only where the reference
  > answer is at most five words, so correctness can be determined. Answers are graded
  > by exact match, then by token rules, then by manual labels where no rule applies.
  > The labels were proposed with LLM assistance and reviewed; they were not set by
  > domain experts.

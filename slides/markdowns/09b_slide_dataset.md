# slide_dataset

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > The benchmark: BioASQ factoids

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > 1,000 short biomedical questions, each with a short reference answer.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Questions from the BioASQ challenges, kept only where a reference answer is at most
  > five words, so right and wrong can be told apart. Answers are graded by exact match,
  > then by token rules, then by manual labels for what no rule can settle. Those labels
  > were proposed with LLM help and checked, not set by domain experts.

# slide_uq_map

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > Where the signal comes from

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > Two properties locate every estimator: the model access it needs and the number of
  > answers it draws.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Position further right indicates greater access required: a hosted API exposes text
  > only, an open model also exposes token probabilities and hidden states. The lower
  > row samples the same question several times. The next slide places every estimator
  > in these families, and the four after it take each family in turn.

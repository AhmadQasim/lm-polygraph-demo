# slide_selective_generation

The three pieces of text the *slide* carries, around the figure. Edit them
here. The lead and the caption flow — they are wrapped by PowerPoint, so
write them over as many `>` lines as reads well and they are joined back into
one paragraph. Titles do not flow.

## the slide

- **title** — the slide's heading, top left
  > What an uncertainty score is for

- **lead** — the blue line under the title — one sentence, it wraps on its own
  > Rank the answers, flag the least certain, and what is kept gets better.

- **caption** — the grey paragraph under the figure — it wraps on its own
  > Accuracy of the answers kept as the most uncertain are flagged for review, ranked by
  > mean token entropy. Flagging half of them lifts the kept accuracy from 31% to 49%. A
  > useless score stays on the flat line; a perfect one follows the upper dashed line.
  > AUROC, used in the results, measures how well the ranking separates right from
  > wrong.
